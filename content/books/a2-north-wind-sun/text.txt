The North Wind and the Sun had an argument. Each one said that he was the strongest. They agreed to test their power on a traveller who was walking home.

"Whoever can make the traveller take off his coat is the winner," said the Sun.

The North Wind went first. He blew with all his might. The sky grew dark, and the trees moved. But the harder the wind blew, the closer the traveller held his coat. The man was cold, and he did not want to lose his only coat.

At last, the North Wind stopped. "I cannot do it," he said.

Then the Sun came out from behind a cloud. He shone warmly on the road. The traveller walked past a house and a field of yellow flowers. Soon he felt hot. He took off his coat and carried it over his arm. Then he sat near the water and drank from his hand.

The Sun smiled. "People listen to a warm friend more often than to a strong wind."
